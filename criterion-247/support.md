For help with the Criterion 24/7 iPhone and iPad app, email [christopher.meiklejohn@gmail.com](mailto:christopher.meiklejohn@gmail.com). Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot can help; please remove any personal information you don't want to share.

### Film information or widgets look out of date

Check your internet connection and open the app again. Film information depends on external services, and iOS controls when widgets refresh, so a widget may update later than the app. Timing can be approximate.

### Notifications aren't arriving

Enable “Tell me when a film starts” in the app's Settings and allow notifications in your device's Settings. Delivery depends on the app refreshing its film information and your device's notification settings. Alerts announce a changeover; they don't predict the next film.

### Watching a film

Use the app's link to open the Criterion Channel. Subscription, playback, and Criterion Channel account questions should go to [Criterion Channel support](https://www.criterionchannel.com/contact/support). This independent companion app cannot manage your Criterion Channel subscription.
