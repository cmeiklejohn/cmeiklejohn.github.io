Collaboration & teaching

Industry collaborations and teaching.

My SyncFree research connected academic groups with Basho, Trifork, Rovio, and Erlang Solutions. I contributed to the successful €3.57M LightKone consortium proposal for edge computing.

At CMU, I teach how to deploy safely and reason about microservice failures. I mentored Lydia Stark’s circuit-breaker research, published at SoCC 2022, and Michael Issac Assad’s database-resilience and developer-tooling research, published as an ICSE 2024 tool demonstration. Earlier, I co-mentored a Google Summer of Code project that led to an AGERE 2016 paper.
