# Writing Guidance

For any public-facing prose in this repository, read
`scripts/voice/voice-guide.md` before drafting or revising.

- Treat Christopher Meiklejohn's authorial pre-2026 blog posts as the primary
  evidence for narrative voice.
- Use sole-authored and first-authored papers for technical precision,
  qualification, and argument structure. Do not infer surface voice from
  papers where Christopher is one of several coauthors.
- Do not use 2026 or later posts to infer voice. That is the boundary after
  which AI-assisted writing entered the archive.
- Analyze every eligible source, but weight recent pre-2026 long-form writing
  more heavily than early announcements, reading notes, or short product
  updates.
- Preserve the distinction between voice and truth. Citation checks, claim
  boundaries, experimental provenance, and uncertainty must be verified
  independently of the prose pass.
- Before any public post is called prose-ready, run the three independent
  editorial gates in `scripts/voice/voice-guide.md`: structural reader,
  evidence reviewer, and continuous-read readability editor. A generic hostile
  review or prose audit does not substitute for these three passes.
- Never copy an old passage into a new post merely because it sounds right.
  Reproduce habits of reasoning and cadence, not distinctive wording.
- Do not rewrite an existing user-authored draft without explicit approval.

The reproducible corpus audit is generated by
`scripts/voice/analyze_corpus.py`; its checked-in snapshot is
`scripts/voice/corpus-report.json`.
